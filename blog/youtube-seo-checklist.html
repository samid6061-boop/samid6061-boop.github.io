Frequently asked questions

How long should a YouTube title be?

The common advice is roughly 60 characters or fewer, with the main phrase near the front, because longer titles can be cut off in some places.

Do YouTube tags still matter?

They play only a minor role now. Time spent on the title, thumbnail, and description usually pays off more than time spent on tags.
