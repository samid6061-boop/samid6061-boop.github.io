Heads-up on length: all four of my real titles above are longer than 60 characters, and they got between 88K and 322K views. A length check tells you what might be cut off, not what will perform. For me, timing and topic mattered more.

How these checks work

  • YouTube rules: a title can be up to 100 characters including spaces, and YouTube doesn't accept the characters < and > in a title.
  • Rules of thumb: about 40 characters are visible in the Shorts feed and about 60 in search. Guides disagree on the exact numbers, and layouts differ by device, so the preview is only rough. Keeping the main phrase near the front, avoiding repeated words, and avoiding all caps are common advice, not YouTube requirements.
  • What it can't check: whether the title is true, whether it names the right person, or whether the video will get views.

Frequently asked questions

How long can a YouTube Shorts title be?

100 characters, including spaces, the same limit as other YouTube videos. Much less than that is visible on screen, so put the important words first.

Does this tool store my title?

No. It runs in your browser. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.
