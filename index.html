From content writer to full digital partner

I started writing content professionally in 2020, based in Pakistan. Five years on, that's grown into SEO strategy, copywriting, digital marketing, and — most recently — AI-assisted creative work and prompt engineering.

Most clients come to me once they're tired of coordinating a writer, a marketer, an editor, and a video person separately. I run that whole chain myself, so the brief only has to be explained once.

What that means day to day: SEO content that's built to rank before it's polished to read well, copy tested against a specific action you want a reader to take, and a marketing plan for where that content actually needs to go.
